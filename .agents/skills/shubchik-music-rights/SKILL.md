---
name: shubchik-music-rights
description: Build or review music matching, provider links and local playback in Shubchik.
---

Keep track metadata, local files and provider links distinct. Use MusicBrainz for recording metadata and comply with its rate limit and client identification requirements. Check provider documentation before adding access to private libraries or embedded playback. Keep playback of user-provided files available offline. Present filename matches as candidates when the identity of the recording is uncertain.
